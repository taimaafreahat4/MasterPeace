THE IDEA

Inventory should help a business move forward—not hold it back.

Merchants often have to make purchasing and pricing decisions with information scattered across products, sales, and stock records. That can make it hard to spot slow-moving items early or understand how much capital is sitting on a shelf.

Stock Mind is designed to bring those signals together. It helps sellers review what they have, understand possible risks, and consider a next step with the evidence in view.

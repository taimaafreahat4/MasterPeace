Why Stock Mind How it works For sellers and buyers

See what’s moving and what’s sitting.

When stock levels, costs, and sales live in different places, it can be hard to know which products to reorder or which ones are tying up cash.

Stock Mind brings those details together. You can review inventory, use sales data to evaluate demand, and list products you have available to sell.

How Stock Mind works

Start with your inventory and decide what to do with each product.

Demand estimates depend on the inventory and sales information you provide.

  1. Review your stock

    Bring product quantities, costs, and sales into one seller workspace.

  2. Spot slow-moving items

    Check which products need attention and review demand estimates when you have recent sales data.

  3. Choose your next step

    Use the available information to evaluate a purchase, review a price, or keep monitoring an item.

  4. List surplus stock

    Offer extra inventory by quantity or as a whole lot so buyers can choose what fits their needs.

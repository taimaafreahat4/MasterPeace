Privacy Policy

This browser based prototype keeps some account, cart, order, notification, and feedback data in your browser storage so that demo workflows can continue between pages. Data saved this way is local to this browser profile and is not automatically sent to a Stock Mind server.

Do not enter real payment credentials or sensitive personal information in this demo. The checkout and delivery flow are demonstrations and do not process a real payment or shipment. Clearing browser site data may remove locally saved demo information.

For questions, contact hello@stockmind.com.

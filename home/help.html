STOCK MIND SUPPORT

How can we help?

Quick guidance for the main workflows in the Stock Mind demo.

How do I find products?

Open Categories to browse available marketplace listings. You can add eligible items to your cart and continue through the demo checkout.

How do I sell my stock?

Sign in or create an account, then open the seller center to manage inventory and marketplace listings. The seller sign in link opens the seller entry point.

Are checkout and delivery real?

No. This version simulates order processing, delivery updates, and feedback in your browser. It does not charge a payment method or dispatch a real shipment.

Where can I see order updates?

Use the order tracking link shown after placing a demo order. Delivery notifications are displayed within the site when supported by the local demo data.

How do I send feedback or ask for help?

Share product feedback through our website feedback form, or email hello@stockmind.com.
